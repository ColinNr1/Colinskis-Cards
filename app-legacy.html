Price QA

Sources

Primary identity: TCGdex v2. Exact card ID, set, local number, images and multilingual card records.

Primary structured pricing: TCGdex provider data when present. Cardmarket and TCGplayer are retained as separate sources and currencies.

Secondary verification/fallback: Pokémon TCG API v2. Its structured card objects may contain Cardmarket and TCGplayer price blocks.

No marketplace search snippets or arbitrary listing prices are treated as market value. Missing data remains Unknown.
